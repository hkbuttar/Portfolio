MACHINE LEARNING I & II

Advanced Machine Learning & AI Coursework

Machine Learning I & Machine Learning II 2025 – 2026

Overview

A ten-assignment sequence covering the core toolkit of modern machine learning and deep learning, from foundational architectures through generative and self-supervised methods, each delivered as a self-contained Colab notebook.

Approach

  • Implemented feedforward networks from first principles (MSE/BCE/softmax losses) and a full MNIST classification pipeline.
  • Built Word2Vec skip-gram embeddings and trained RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs on Tiny Shakespeare for character-level generation.
  • Implemented VAEs, GANs, and DCGANs for image generation, and SimCLR for contrastive self-supervised representation learning.
  • Applied transfer learning with ResNet-18 on CIFAR-10 and evaluated multiple pretrained architectures on ImageNet.
