PROJECT 01

Retail Demand Forecasting & PEFT Benchmarking

Rakuten Advertising · Capstone Jan 2026 – Aug 2026 with Kevin Fan, Aren Mizuno, Kai Schindler

Overview

A capstone project built with Rakuten Advertising comparing classical, machine-learning, and foundation-model approaches to retail demand forecasting, alongside parameter-efficient fine-tuning techniques, across receipt-level NIQ data spanning Luxury Brand Retail, Luxury Department Retail, and Travel verticals.

Approach

  • Benchmarked SARIMA, SARIMAX, ETS, GARCH, and VAR against LightGBM, XGBoost, and foundation time-series models (Chronos, TimesFM, TTM, MOMENT, Moirai).
  • Evaluated PEFT fine-tuning techniques — LoRA, QLoRA, DoRA, BitFit, P-Tuning, Prompt Tuning, and Prefix Tuning — for adapting foundation models to retail-specific patterns.
  • Built a Vite + React PWA dashboard with multi-model comparison charts, confidence bands, residual plots, metric toggles, and CSV/PNG export, backed by a FastAPI service with GZip compression.
  • Authored the methodology and conclusion sections of the capstone paper and produced the metrics slide for the final presentation deck.
