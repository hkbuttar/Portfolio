PROJECT 09

Ingredient Match

Independent Project 2025 – 2026 Solo

Overview

A cross-retailer beauty product recommender that matches products by their actual ingredient lists rather than category or brand alone — given any product, it surfaces the most ingredient-similar alternatives across Sephora and Ulta.

Approach

  • Built resumable Selenium scrapers (undetected-chromedriver) for Sephora and Ulta that crawl every brand page, extract ingredients from each product's embedded __NEXT_DATA__ JSON with DOM/regex fallbacks, and save incrementally so a run can be safely interrupted and resumed.
  • Cleaned both raw datasets — dropping products with missing ingredients, fixing malformed line terminators and unquoted ingredient cells, and stripping marketing blurbs and shade-name prefixes from ingredient text.
  • Combined and deduplicated the two retailers into one dataset: normalized 261 raw category names down to ~55 canonical categories, then used rapidfuzz token-sort matching on brand + product name to identify cross-retailer duplicates.
  • Built the ranking logic itself — exact ingredient-set overlap as the primary score, Jaccard similarity as a tiebreak (to avoid rewarding products that merely have very long ingredient lists), and rating as a secondary tiebreak — surfaced through an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
