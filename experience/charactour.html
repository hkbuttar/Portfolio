EXPERIENCE 02

Data Science Project Manager

CharacTour, LLC Jun 2025 – Sep 2025 Chicago, IL

Overview

Directed a team of three data science interns at CharacTour, using data from 10M+ consumers to map 23 personality traits to character and entertainment preferences, powering identity-driven AI voice and recommendation systems.

Approach

  • Directed a team of three data science interns analyzing 10M+ consumer records to map 23 personality traits to entertainment preferences, powering identity-driven AI recommendations.
  • Built NLP pipelines detecting cluster-specific speech patterns — sentence structure, emotional cadence — enabling human-level nuance in AI-generated voices and recommendations.
  • Designed an evaluation framework comparing AI-generated dialogue against real character dialogue and TikTok speech patterns to improve naturalness and relatability.
  • Created a dynamic language-mapping framework analyzing TikTok scripts to identify the vocabulary, tone, and structure that resonated with distinct audience segments.
  • Built a scalable diarization system for long-form video — automatically slicing, transcribing, and assigning character identity across scenes — cutting manual tagging effort by 80%.
  • Drove improvements to Named Entity Recognition to extract and attribute quotes across complex narrative contexts, keeping character attribution consistent across massive entertainment datasets.
